How it works

      
    
        
  • Energy use = each device's power × hours in each state (scanning, idle, after hours). Scanner powers come from a published measurement study where a model was measured; otherwise you enter them.
    • 
        
  • Solar = yearly (grid kept) or worst-month (off-grid) energy ÷ modelled PV yield from EC JRC PVGIS 5.3 for the chosen point, which already includes 14% system losses. Battery losses are included only if you enter the round-trip efficiency.
    • 
        
  • Battery = backup hours × average open-hours load, or days of autonomy × daily energy. Module count needs your battery and inverter datasheets.
    • 
        
  • Budget is the installed quote you enter. Savings, payback and avoided CO₂ are not calculated in this release: monthly energy totals do not establish when solar can serve the load or whether the battery can shift it. Grid factors are provided as dated source references.
    • 
        
  • Nothing here is a measured saving, a guaranteed backup time or a demonstrated patient benefit. The OCH pilot will measure energy, fuel, interruptions and patient waiting after it opens.
    • 
      

      
Prepared for the RSNA 2026 Imaging Sustainability Challenge. Reference data, method and checks are in the project repository.
